Zero-Disk Enterprise Privacy Guarantee

CardLyze enforces strict data confidentiality designed for organizational security:

100% In-Memory Processing Uploaded files and datasets are streamed directly into RAM. No temporary files or caches are ever written to disk.
Ephemeral Session Scoping All records are bound to short-lived sessions that automatically expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and wipe on tab close.
Hardened Security Headers Every response includes restrictive Content-Security-Policy, anti-caching, and no-referrer protection headers.
